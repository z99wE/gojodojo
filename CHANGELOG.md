# Changelog

All notable changes to GojoDojo by Codebenders are documented in this file.

## [2.1.0] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- Real vehicle driving laws: manual throttle acceleration, active braking with illuminated rear brake lights, and continuous lateral steering.
- Off-road surface friction: driving off-road on grass/dirt shoulders limits speed to 40 km/h and generates kick-up particles.
- Physical vehicle collisions: rival vehicle impacts and roadside guardrail crashes cause screen shake, metal crunches, spark bursts, and shield loss.
- Complete physiological study anxiety model: Pulse BPM meter (70-170 BPM), procedural audio heartbeat pulses, and high-anxiety red screen vignettes.
- Pranayama Zen Breath mechanic (B key): triggers vagus nerve regulation, resets heart rate to 72 BPM, and enables bullet-time slow motion for 3.5 seconds.
- Study angle custom topic selector: allows typing or speaking any custom topic or chapter for dynamic quiz synthesis.
- Crystal-clear Devanagari typography: integrated Mukta and Noto Sans Devanagari, replacing illegible pixel fonts with high-contrast text.
- Speech recognition voice input: voice topic selection and spoken quiz answering in Hindi and English.
- Bundled screen reader TTS: reads questions, options, and explanations on demand.
- In-browser AI STEM Pit Crew (Guru Bot): answers student doubts with everyday analogies and anxiety reduction techniques.
